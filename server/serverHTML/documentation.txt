This is documentation of functions you can use:
<<<Not every detail is documented here because it can reveal some stuff in game>>>

1. is_pressed(key):
    returns True if key is pressed on your
    keyboard and False if key isn't pressed
    example: 
        if is_pressed('w') == True:
            forward()

2. turn_right():
    turns your player right

3. turn_left():
    turns your player left

4. forward():
    moves your player forward

5. turn_towards(direction):
    turns your player in specific direction.
    directions: up - 'north'
                down - 'south'
                left - 'west'
                right - 'east'
    example:
        turn_towards('east')

6. get_position():
    returns your player's current position in whole map

7. interact(action):
    There are more parameters you can
    write into this function and
    here is every parameter that you can use explained.

    A. interact('show'):
        returns information about block.
        Works on just some blocks,
        explore your world to find out more!

    B. interact('put', item_name):
        puts item into chests or other
        blocks from your inventory

    C. interact('take', item_name):
        takes item from chest to your inventory

    D. interact('craft', final_crafting_item_name):
        takes all needed resources from
        your inventory and crafts item

    E. interact('open'):
        opens treasures or other stuff

8. attack():
    attacks enemies in your range.

9. equip(item_name, body_slot_name):
    equips an equippable item from your
    inventory into your equipment slots

10. unequip(body_slot_name):
    unequips item at the slot and
    puts it into your inventory

11. set_skin(skin_chars):
    sets skin of your player based on chars
    you enter(uses ASCII table to
    convert chars into colors)

12. stop_actions():
    clears all actions.
    For example if you press
    forward 5 times in a row,
    it saves all 5 presses in a buffer and
    they execute one after each other but
    if you don't want to go forward anymore
    but in your buffer are still actions,
    you can clear all actions with this
    function to clear your buffer

13. get_map():
    returns 2D field of map around the player.
    (Size of field is possible to
    enlarge by having bigger sight)
    Explore this function in your code to
    know the structure of this 2D field and
    you can use this function to create autofinding bots.
